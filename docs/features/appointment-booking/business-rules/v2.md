# Online booking · business rules v2

Date: 2026-10-05. Basis: meeting 2026-10-01, round 1 answers (Ana and Carla).

No provisional rules: all are settled.

## 1. What the feature is

The pet owner books a consultation or a vaccine for their pet on the website, picking a free slot of a vet's schedule. The front desk sees the day's agenda, marks who attended or did not show up, and handles what falls outside the rules: fit in appointments, emergencies, rescheduling.

## 2. Why

- The front desk spends the morning on the phone just to book.
- The pet owner can only book during business hours.
- The clinic does not know who misses appointments often.

## 3. Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| pet owner | the pet's owner, the person who books |
| schedule | each vet's working hours in the week |
| free slot | a stretch of the schedule with no booking, as long as the service |
| fit in | an appointment outside the schedule, only the front desk books it |
| no show | the pet owner did not come to a confirmed appointment |

## 4. Journey

1. The pet owner picks the pet and the service.
2. The pet owner picks a free slot.
3. The pet owner confirms.
4. The pet owner sees or cancels their own appointments.
5. The front desk sees the day's agenda and marks attendance or no show.

## 5. Business rules

RN-n (business rule).

### 5.1 Booking

- RN-01 Only a pet owner with an account (email and phone) can book. [meeting 01:05]
- RN-02 The website shows only free slots of the schedule. [meeting 01:30]
- RN-03 A consultation takes 30 min; a vaccine takes 15 min. [meeting 01:30]
- RN-04 The minimum lead time to book is 2 h. [Ana (A4); Carla (A4)]
- RN-05 Each pet has at most 2 future appointments. [Ana (A3); Carla (A3)]
- RN-06 A booking is confirmed when made. [Carla (A2)]

### 5.2 Cancelling

- RN-07 The pet owner cancels on their own up to 12 h before. [Carla (A1)]
- RN-08 After the deadline, only the front desk cancels. [meeting 03:47]

### 5.3 Off the website

- RN-09 Fit in appointments and emergencies are not booked on the website; the screen shows the clinic's phone number. [meeting 02:16, 09:15]

### 5.4 Day's agenda

- RN-10 The front desk marks each appointment of the day as attended or no show. [Carla (B1)]

## 6. States

```
confirmed → cancelled   (pet owner within the deadline, or front desk)
confirmed → attended    (front desk)
confirmed → no show     (front desk)
```

| State | Who sees it | Who acts |
|---|---|---|
| confirmed | pet owner, front desk | pet owner (cancels within the deadline), front desk |
| cancelled | pet owner, front desk | nobody |
| attended | pet owner, front desk | nobody |
| no show | front desk | nobody |

## 7. Who does what

| Action | Pet owner | Front desk |
|---|---|---|
| book a free slot | yes | yes |
| book a fit in | no | yes |
| cancel within the deadline | yes | yes |
| cancel after the deadline | no | yes |
| mark attended or no show | no | yes |
