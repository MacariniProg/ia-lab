# Veterinary clinics: terms

| Term | Meaning | Not to confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| pet owner | the person responsible for the pet, who books and pays | the pet (the patient) |
| fit in | an appointment squeezed in outside the schedule | emergency |
| emergency | a pet that needs care now; seen on arrival, never waits for a slot | fit in |
| no show | the pet owner did not come to a confirmed appointment | a cancellation |
