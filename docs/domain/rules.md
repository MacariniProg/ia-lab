# Veterinary clinics: domain rules

RD-n (Regra de Domínio). Each rule: what holds, why, source.

## Personal data

- RD-1 A pet owner's name and phone are used only for what they were given for (booking and reaching them about it). Why: purpose principle of the LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, Brazil's data protection law), article 6, I. [https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm]
- RD-2 Only people who need a pet owner's data for their work see it; a pet owner sees only their own. Why: security principle of the LGPD, article 6, VII. [https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm]
