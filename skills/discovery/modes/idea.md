# Mode: idea (no meeting)

There is no record of what was asked, only the idea. This mode interviews you until the request is clear enough to write down, then checks it the same way meeting mode does.

0. **Interview.** Rounds of up to 4 questions, each with options and one recommended (house rules). Infer what the idea already says; ask only what it does not. Cover, in this order, and stop as soon as each is clear:
   - **who** uses it and who asks for it;
   - **problem:** what goes wrong today without it;
   - **done when:** what the user can do once it exists;
   - **out:** what it is not, so the scope does not grow;
   - **why now:** the deadline or event behind it, if any.
   A question you cannot answer either (a product or business decision) is not asked again: it becomes a question for product. Answers the user gives are cited as `interview <date>`. Done when every bullet above is answered or turned into a question for product.
1. **Already settled** and **what exists:** same as steps 1 and 2 of `modes/meeting.md`.
2. **Write `understanding.md`** from `templates/understanding.md`, citing the interview instead of meeting times.
